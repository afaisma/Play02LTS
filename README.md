# ReadingBuddy

**ReadingBuddy** is an interactive reading app that helps children enjoy stories with narrated audio and highlighted text. Stories are presented one page at a time with illustrations, optional read-aloud narration, and simple navigation.

---

## What It Does

- **Library of stories** — Browse a catalog of stories and books (e.g. fairy tales, rhymebooks, counting, and themed stories). Each book opens to a full-screen reading experience.
- **Read-aloud with highlighting** — When narration is on, the app plays audio and highlights the words in sync so children can follow along. You can choose human narration (recorded voice), computer voice, or no voice (silent reading).
- **Page-by-page reading** — Each story is split into pages. You move forward and back with buttons or by swiping on the text or picture area. Your place in each book is saved so you can continue later.
- **Illustrations and layout** — Every page can show a main image or a small gallery of images, with text and optional background art. Layout and styling can vary by story.
- **Categories and discovery** — The library can be filtered by category (e.g. fairy tales, family, science, manners, nature) so you can find stories by theme or age.
- **Extra features** — The app can include a map or home screen to reach the library, a bookstore area, settings (e.g. reading speed, voice), and parent-oriented information.

## How to Use It

1. **Open the app**  I clike
   You’ll see an initial screen (e.g. a map or library). Tap or use the interface to go to the **Library**.

2. **Pick a book**  
   In the Library, browse the list of books. You can use category filters if available. Tap a book’s cover or title to open it.

3. **Read the story**  
   - The first page of the story appears with text and image(s).  
   - If narration is on, tap play (or it may start automatically) to hear the page read aloud while words are highlighted.  
   - Use **Next** / **Prev** (or swipe left/right on the text or image) to turn pages.  
   - Your progress is saved; next time you open that book, you can continue from where you left off.

4. **Adjust voice and behavior**  
   - In the story view, use the voice options to switch between **human**, **computer**, or **no voice**.  
   - If there is an autoplay or “advance to next page when done” option, you can turn it on so the app moves to the next page after the narration finishes.

5. **Leave a story**  
   Use **Home** (or the equivalent) to return to the Library or main screen and choose another book.

6. **Settings and parents**  
   If the app includes Settings or a Parents section, use them to change reading speed, language, or to read parent notes and information about the stories.

## Who It’s For

ReadingBuddy is aimed at **children and families** who want:

- A simple, book-like experience on phone or tablet  
- Optional read-aloud with word highlighting to support early and reluctant readers  
- A curated set of stories (including classics and educational titles)  
- Progress that is saved per book so reading can be done in short sessions  

---

## Summary

ReadingBuddy is an interactive reading app that lets children browse a library of stories, read page by page with illustrations, and optionally listen to narration with synchronized word highlighting. You choose books from the library, turn pages with buttons or swipes, and can switch between human voice, computer voice, or silent reading. Progress is saved so readers can continue later. The app is designed for at-home or on-the-go reading with a focus on clarity and ease of use.
